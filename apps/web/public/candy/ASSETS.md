# Illustrations Snackpress

Création le 15 octobre 2026 avec l’outil intégré `image_gen.imagegen`, en mode création, `transparent_background: true`. Aucun visuel ou personnage d’un jeu existant n’a été fourni ou copié.

Fichiers utilisés dans l’application :

- `hero.webp` : illustration du bandeau, dérivée du PNG généré de 1536 × 1024 pixels ; WebP qualité 88 avec alpha préservé.
- `snacks.webp` : planche de sprites 4 × 4, dérivée du PNG généré de 1254 × 1254 pixels ; WebP qualité 92 avec alpha préservé. Affichage par positionnement CSS, sans modification de l’illustration.

## Prompt du héros

Create a polished original 3D candy-mobile-game illustration for a French print-tools app called Snackpress. Asset: hero illustration, transparent background, landscape composition. A delightful chunky cheeseburger in warm golden brioche bun with glossy tomato and lettuce, a pink frosted sprinkle donut leaning next to it, a little orange pizza slice, a cute rounded salmon sushi maki, plus two small translucent turquoise and purple jelly candies and a few golden four-point candy sparkles. Arrange as one balanced clustered still-life with the burger left-center and donut right-center, little snacks in foreground. High-end cheerful casual puzzle game art, glossy soft molded edible materials, inflated rounded silhouettes, pink/purple soft studio lighting, rich saturated coral, raspberry, turquoise, caramel gold. Soft grounded shadows but genuinely transparent surroundings. Front three-quarter view, objects occupy most of image, generous safe margin around every edge. No text, no numbers, no logo, no faces, no existing game characters, no UI, no environment or background. Beautiful production-ready game art, not flat vector, not photographic food.

Sixteen equal square cells, no visible grid, no labels, each object centered exactly in its own cell with 15% padding; all objects separate, no overlap between cells, consistent scale and front three-quarter camera. Each icon looks like a polished premium casual candy puzzle game collectible, with smooth inflated silhouettes, luscious glossy highlights, vivid candy colors and soft shadows. Bright, friendly, beautifully modeled, appetizing toy-like food, not flat vector or emoji. Every icon must remain completely inside its cell, centered, legible at small sizes. No letters, no numbers, no frames, no characters, no faces, no logos, no watermark. Uniform transparent background, square output.
